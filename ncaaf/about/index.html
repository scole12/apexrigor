The Operation

APEX NCAA Football is one point-in-time forecasting operation with two full-game engines: ATS at the authentic FanDuel spread and Totals at the authentic FanDuel total.

NCAA Football — Full-Game ATS · Full-Game Totals

Full-Game ATS — Bet-All

Every Division I game with an eligible authentic FanDuel full-game spread receives one immutable Favorite or Underdog position. The engine forecasts a complete Favorite-minus-Underdog margin distribution; home/away identity and moneyline data are not predictive inputs.

Full-Game Totals — Bet-All

Every game with an eligible authentic FanDuel full-game total receives one Over or Under position. A complete integer scoring distribution preserves exact push probability before the posted total is applied.

Model Framework

Active engine identities are read from the live production manifest at issuance, so the public card binds each forecast to its exact model hash. Development uses chronological evaluation and independent reproduction; a later result can never replace a point-in-time input.

Production Process

Each game is prepared at exactly T-3, three hours before kickoff. At T-2, two hours before kickoff, lawful state and authentic FanDuel markets are refreshed and the eligible forecasts are issued immutably. One 7:00 AM America/New_York grader settles every prior-gameday position against its originally issued line.

Market Philosophy

The actual FanDuel spread and total define the propositions. Sportsbook prices support factual display, settlement, economics and audit; they are not football-model features. No moneyline model is operated.

Conviction Framework

Operating Principles

Responsible Use

APEX publishes quantitative forecasts for informational and research purposes. No model guarantees outcomes, and all betting involves risk.
