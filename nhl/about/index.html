Two pregame engines: full-game Totals O/U and underdog +1.5 puck lines.

Totals use a bet-all policy: one Over or Under for every eligible game with a captured FanDuel total. Totals are not filtered by confidence or edge.

Puck-line selections are limited to verified underdogs that pass a filter established in the isolated NHL testing authority. The issued card retains the captured line and price.

NHL picks are currently UNISSUED. Results will show the selections and confidence tiers recorded when picks are issued.

The all-sports banner shows the Total Apex record. NHL Overall, market, tier and slate records are calculated only from sealed NHL issuances and their grades.
