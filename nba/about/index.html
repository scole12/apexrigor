APEX NBA

The Operation

APEX NBA is a two-engine quantitative forecasting program covering full-game point spreads and totals. Each eligible game receives one against-the-spread selection and one Over or Under selection at the authentic FanDuel market. Every issued position is retained and graded; the bet-all program does not select only favorable-looking games or publish moneyline picks.

The Two Engines

Full-Game Spread | ATS Shrink C3

The spread engine estimates each team's probability of covering the posted full-game handicap. It combines player-impact estimates, projected playing time, rotation and matchup context with a market-informed shrinkage calculation. The side with the higher modeled cover probability is issued, whether favorite or underdog. The prediction is evaluated against the original FanDuel spread and price, not a later line.

Full-Game Totals | D4 Ratings Lag-1

The totals engine evaluates the probability of finishing above or below the posted full-game total. Its inputs include team and player efficiency ratings, projected scoring, expected minutes, rotation availability and lagged ratings. The lagged inputs are designed to preserve the information timeline. The engine issues the higher-probability Over or Under position at the captured total and price.

Research and Data Integrity

APEX integrates historical results, player and team performance, lineup and injury context, projected minutes and authentic market observations. Event timestamps and data-availability timestamps distinguish what happened from what was knowable before a forecast. The two champion engines retain separate mathematical configurations and historical evaluation records.

Model candidates are compared chronologically using prediction error, probability accuracy and reliability against the market. A leading research candidate is not automatically evidence of a profitable live advantage; production performance is tracked independently.

Publication and Accountability

The scheduled process refreshes available data and issues a sealed pregame card at the established T-minus-six horizon. The original selection, probability, FanDuel line, price and confidence tier remain associated with each position for next-day grading. Every eligible issued game is accounted for, including losses and pushes, without substituting in-game or closing odds.

Weak, Moderate, Strong and Elite are ordered relative-confidence classifications within each engine. They describe the model's forecast ranking, not guaranteed results or a claim that each historical band has demonstrated a distinct realized winning rate. The published Results record is intended to make that performance transparent.

Responsible Use

APEX publishes quantitative forecasts for research and informational purposes. Forecasts are uncertain, betting prices carry costs, and no model guarantees profit or a winning outcome.
